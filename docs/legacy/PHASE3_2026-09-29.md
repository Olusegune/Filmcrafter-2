# Phase 3: Performance profiles and micro-animation (29 Sep 2026)

## What was built

- **Five acting profiles** in the new **Acting** sheet (left rail):

  | Profile | Style |
  |---|---|
  | Natural | truthful, small motivated gestures |
  | Restrained | internal; the feeling is in the eyes and breath |
  | Expressive | open, readable, grounded |
  | Dramatic | heightened, held beats |
  | Energetic | rhythmic, performing to camera |

- **Where a profile is set:** for the whole video, per character or per scene (in the sheet), and per shot (in the shot panel). When several apply, the order of precedence is shot, then scene, then character, then video.
- **In prompts:**
  - The profile replaces the fixed "play it truthfully" line with its own acting direction.
  - It scales the shot's directed intensity. For example, 6/10 played Restrained becomes about 4/10.
  - Video prompts get the profile's micro-animation (blinks, breathing, weight shifts, hair and fabric). Keyframe prompts get the matching expression and pose.
- **Micro-animation on moving stills (free).** A still of people gently breathes and sways, sized by the profile (Restrained barely moves; Energetic moves faster). This happens in both the export (ffmpeg) and the live preview (CSS). Shots without people don't breathe.
- **Opt-in.** Projects that never pick a profile keep exactly the prompts they had.

## Evidence

- **Tests:** 122 engine tests pass, 6 of them new. They cover:
  - precedence order;
  - prompt text for video vs still;
  - unchanged prompts without a profile;
  - validation and clearing;
  - a real ffmpeg render whose frames differ over time.
- **Installed app, QA Flow Song:**
  - The Acting sheet set the video to Restrained and Sege to Expressive.
  - The free prompt preview of S01-01 carries Expressive acting and micro-motion for Sege.
  - A moving still on S01-01 breathes in the preview while playing (the transform changes over time).
  - A full export of the project with the micro-motion completed in 72 s.

## Known limitations

- Micro-animation on stills is a whole-frame breath and sway, not per-body motion. Real blinks and gestures come from video renders, which the prompts now direct.
- A profile never re-renders existing takes. It applies to new renders only.

## Gate

Profiles are chosen, saved, resolved and delivered into prompts, the preview and the export on Windows.

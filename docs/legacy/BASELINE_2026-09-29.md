## Persistence and restart

- Jobs, approvals and the library are written to disk on every change. The job store is lock-protected and merges field by field.
- Jobs still in flight when the app closed are picked up by the renderer on the next launch (verified earlier with real renders).
- A job that was mid-send when the app closed is marked "uncertain" and is never re-sent automatically.

## Known limitations

- The Mac build has not been run on hardware.
- The meaning of fal's negative balance is undocumented.
- The keyframe check needs OpenAI or a local vision model.
- Remaking the 12 failed Exit Plan keyframes costs money and needs your go-ahead.

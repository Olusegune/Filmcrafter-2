# Phase 5: Production Supervisor QA (29 Sep 2026)

## What was built

The **Supervisor** has its own button in the left rail. Every quality finding sits in one queue.

- **Sources:**
  - the review of each shot's take in use (major and minor issues with their corrections);
  - the keyframe check of each approved keyframe;
  - continuity, from both the free rules and the AI cross-shot review;
  - failed renders, and renders whose status is unknown (these may have been charged);
  - shots still without a take, grouped into one note;
  - takes never visually checked, grouped into one note.
- **Decisions per finding:**
  - **Review** opens the shot.
  - **Accept** means it's fine as it is. Accepting a take review also marks the take accepted, so Review and Autopilot stop flagging it.
  - **Ignore** means it's not a problem.
  - **Fix** re-renders with the finding's correction.
  - **Regenerate** re-renders as planned.
  - **Reopen** undoes a decision.
- **Fix and Regenerate** use the project's mode pick (Plan & budget). They show the price and send only on *Send*. They go through the normal queue, so Local First and the project budget apply. A finding being re-rendered shows *Re-rendering…*, then *Re-rendered* or *Re-render failed*.
- **Release readiness:** "Release ready" once no major finding is open, and a count of minor findings, notes and decisions.
- **Persistence:** decisions are kept in `supervisor.json`. A finding tied to a take or keyframe that has since been replaced drops out, and the new one is judged fresh.
- **Budget:** unpublished prices now count at $0.25 per still (was $0.10), so GPT Image calls are never under-counted.

## Evidence

- **Tests:** 138 engine tests pass, 7 of them new. They cover:
  - collection from all sources;
  - Accept marking the take review;
  - Ignore being remembered and Reopen undoing it;
  - Fix queueing with the correction as a fix note;
  - Fix refused under Local First;
  - own media never flagged.
- **Installed app, Exit Plan copy:**
  - 17 major, 28 minor and 4 notes were listed (for example, Sege's overshirt in S01-03 and S01-09, the extra title text in S01-10, and screen direction in S02-02).
  - Ignore moved an item to decided.
  - Fix showed "Re-render with the correction on openai (gpt-image-2.5-flare)" with its price line, and was cancelled.
  - Reopen restored it.
  - Nothing was sent, and the project was left with no decisions.

## Known limitations

- Fix and Regenerate always use the mode's Auto model. To use a specific model, re-render from the shot panel.
- Continuity fixes re-make the keyframe; the video from it is a separate step (Autopilot or Render).

## Gate

The QA queue, decisions, gated re-renders and persistence work in the installed app.

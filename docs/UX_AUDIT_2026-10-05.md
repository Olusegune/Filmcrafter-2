# UI/UX audit, 2026-10-05 (version 1.13.0)

**Scope and method.** Home and the Studio (with the Layers panel open) were run in the browser preview (`npm run dev`, open `/?mock`) at 1440x900 and inspected by eye and with measurements from the page (control sizes, text sizes, unnamed controls). The other screens (wizard, Producer, Shows, Export, Review, Settings) were **not** walked through on screen; findings for them come from reading the code and are marked *(code only)*. Treat those as leads to check, not confirmed defects.

Severity: **High** blocks or misleads, **Medium** slows people down, **Low** polish.

## Fixed in this pass

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **High.** Letter shortcuts ignored modifier keys: `Ctrl+R` opened the re-render sheet, `Ctrl+A` approved a take, `Ctrl+F` toggled full screen, `Ctrl+M` muted. | Plain keys now never match while Ctrl, Cmd or Alt is held (`src/shortcuts.ts`, tested). |
| F2 | **Medium.** Shortcuts fired while a drop-down (`select`) had focus, and `Space` on a focused button played the video as well as pressing the button, so keyboard users could not press buttons. | `select` and editable text count as typing; `Space` on a button, link or summary belongs to that control. |
| F3 | **Medium.** The shortcut sheet (`?`), the key handler and the documentation were three separate lists and had drifted (no J/K/L, nothing about Layers). | One registry drives all three; a test fails if the documentation misses a shortcut. |
| F4 | **Medium.** The scrub bar and the volume bar were 4 px tall, which is very hard to grab. | Same look, 24 px grab area, visible keyboard focus. |
| F5 | **Medium.** If the engine answered "nothing" to a keyframe or layer query, the whole Studio showed "Something went wrong on this screen". | Those answers are now treated as empty. (Found because the browser preview's fake engine returned nothing.) |
| F6 | **Low.** The browser preview (`/?mock`) crashed on opening the sample project. | The fake engine now answers the board, layer and keyframe calls. |

## Open findings

### High
- **H1. The highlighted button is always "Direct My Video".** *(Corrected: an earlier version of this note said two orange buttons competed; only Direct was orange.)* After a video is directed, the next step is Render, yet Direct stayed the orange button, and **Direct…** beside it did not say what it does. **Fixed in the follow-up:** the orange button is the next step (Direct before a plan exists, Render after), Direct becomes a plain button once Render is available, and **Direct…** is now **Ask the Director**.
- **H2. The Layers panel floated over the Project inspector** (fixed, right side, 320 px) and hid it. **Fixed in the follow-up:** it now lives in the inspector column (the shot inspector stays underneath with its tab kept), and layer rows are taller.

### Medium
- **M1. Small text.** Measured on the Studio screen: rail labels and timeline lyrics were 10.5 px, section tags and field titles 11 px. **Partly fixed in the follow-up:** those are now 11.5 to 12 px. Still small by design: count badges, the `$` markers, some chips (10 to 11 px). A full pass over the other screens has not been done.
- **M2. Small hit areas.** Layer rows are now at least 36 px tall and the scrub and volume bars have a 24 px grab area. The tick boxes (18 px) and the up/down arrows in layer rows are unchanged.
- **M3. Rail buttons.** **Project** was greyed out with the tooltip "coming soon", and other disabled buttons did not say why. **Fixed in the follow-up:** Project opens the project page in the inspector, and disabled buttons say "Needs a shot plan first: press Direct My Video." (The 9 px labels are under M1.)
- **M4. The Character name box had no accessible name.** **Fixed in the follow-up** (`aria-label`).
- **M5. The welcome tour card sits over the top-left of the player.** Downgraded to Low: it is anchored under the progress strip it describes and the player is empty at that point. Not changed.
- **M6. No undo for layer edits.** **Fixed in the follow-up:** Undo and Redo in the Layers panel and `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Shift+Z`, `Ctrl+Y`, for the last 40 changes. The history is kept by the engine (`cache/undo-layers.json`), so it covers lyric layers, templates and styles too.
- **M7 (withdrawn).** I said the Studio has no running total of spend. That was wrong: the progress strip shows `$spent / $budget` when the engine reports it (`GoldenStrip.tsx`). The browser preview does not supply it, which is why I did not see it.

### Low
- **L0.** The top bar's inspector button sat over the left end of the inspector header and covered the title's first letter. **Fixed in the follow-up** (measured: the button spans x 1047–1079 at 1440 wide, the inspector starts at 1040).
- **L1.** The empty player shows the shot's description and its framing separately ("Extreme close-up of Sege's closed eye..." and "extreme close-up"). On a second look this is a title and a label, not a duplicate; left as it is.
- **L2.** Reduced motion: the micro-animations already respected it; the player's layer movements did not. **Fixed in the follow-up:** with "reduce motion" set on the computer, the player shows layers still, at rest (keyframed layers still follow their keys). The export is not affected.

## Second pass: the other screens (browser preview, 1440x900, sample data)

`PF_OUT=<folder> node scripts/docshots.mjs export review settings library supervisor acting continuity colour clean directions select wizard produce show makesong docs` captures them all and prints any error the page logs.

**Looked at on screen:** Export, Review, Settings, the new-project wizard (which is also what **Create from song** opens), the New show dialog, the Reference library (it only showed "Loading..." with the sample data) and the in-app Documentation.
**Captured with no page error, but not looked at:** Acting, Clean up, Colour match, Directions, Make a song.
**Could not be audited:** Supervisor, Continuity and Select & review crash with the preview's fake engine, which answers with nothing for the data they read (the real engine does not). They need a richer fake before they can be looked at.

What was found:
- **Good:** Export, Review and the New show dialog are clear, with one obvious action each and plain wording. The Documentation viewer shows the pictures well.
- **Fixed, wizard:** the disabled **Next** gave no reason. It now says "Choose your song to continue." (and, on later steps, to finish or skip the step). The step dots already had names for screen readers; I had guessed they did not.
- **Fixed, Settings:** a program fault (the preview's empty answer) was shown raw as "Cannot use 'in' operator to search for 'providers' in null". Faults in the program itself are now worded for people ("Something unexpected happened on this screen. Your project is safe...") with the technical text kept for a bug report. The engine's own messages are unchanged.
- **Fixed, Documentation:** block keys were line numbers, and two blocks could get the same one. React warned about it, and a repeated key can drop or double a block. It happened in section 4 once the Studio picture was added. Keys are now a running number, and a test fails on repeats (checked against the old keys).
- **Open, Settings:** the sheet is titled "Connections" but is opened from "Settings". Pick one name.
- **Open, small grey text:** the sub-labels under the Export format cards and some hints look pale and are 11 px. I judged this by eye and did not measure contrast; measure before changing.
- **Open, Export:** nothing on the sheet says what the film's sound will be. The loudness now appears after an export; a one-line "Sound: your song, plus the sound you kept on N shots" before it would help.

## Ideas, by value

1. **Command palette.** *Done in the follow-up:* `Ctrl+Shift+P` is one search box for every Studio action and every shot, with the shortcut shown beside each action.
2. **State-based primary action** (H1). *Done.*
3. **Docked Layers panel with undo** (H2, M6). *Done.*
4. **Spend chip.** *Withdrawn:* it exists (M7).
5. **Reorder the shot list.** *Withdrawn:* each shot sits on a moment of the song, so reordering is not meaningful. A filter (needs attention, no video, locked) would be, and is not built.
6. **Loudness meter and ducking** of the song under dialogue. *Ducking is done* as part of the per-shot sound layers (the song drops to 40% automatically under a shot's own sound); a loudness meter is not built.

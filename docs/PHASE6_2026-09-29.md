# Phase 6: Worldwide creative-direction library (29 Sep 2026)

## What was built

- **Directions** has its own button in the left rail. The "How should this song look?" screen also links to it.
- **29 curated directions** from Africa, the Middle East & North Africa, Asia, Europe, the Americas, Oceania, and global forms. Examples:
  - Africa: Lagos Nights, Nollywood Melodrama, Sahel Golden Hour, Amapiano House Party, Afrofuturist Epic
  - Middle East & North Africa: Cairo Golden Age
  - Asia: Bollywood Spectacle, K-pop Concept, Hong Kong Mood Piece, Anime Opening, Pastoral Wonder, Wuxia Poetry, Tropical Dreamlike
  - Europe: French New Wave, Nordic Noir, Neorealist Streets, UK Estates & Grime, Constructivist Montage, Flamenco Duende
  - Americas: Reggaetón Heat, Telenovela Gloss, Carnaval Colour, Magic Realism, 90s Hip-Hop, Americana Road Movie, Dancehall Yard
  - Oceania: Country and Sky, Ocean Voyager
  - Global: Black-and-White Poem, One-Take Performance, Handmade Stop Motion
- **Each direction is a working brief:** camera, light, palette, editing rhythm, movement, design, the genres, tempo and energy it suits, a suggested look and acting profile, what to avoid, and a note on using it with care. The directions describe film-making traditions, not peoples. Where a tradition belongs to a community (for example First Nations Australia, Pacific tattooing or flamenco), the note says to work with that community.
- **"Best for this song"** ranks the directions by tempo (half-time and double-time feels count and are labelled), energy and section moods. It is free and needs no AI.
- **"Use this direction"** does four things; each one can be switched off:
  - writes the brief used by "Show me three directions" and the treatment;
  - fills the Production Bible (world, palette, never show);
  - sets the look;
  - sets the acting profile.

  Your own look, acting and bible entries are kept unless you tick "Replace". A later direction replaces what an earlier direction filled in, but never what you typed.
- **Save my own…** adds your own directions to your library, in `~/.pulseframe/directions.json`. They can be deleted.

## Evidence

- **Tests:** 146 engine tests pass, 9 of them new. They cover:
  - library completeness (every look and acting profile exists, all regions are present);
  - song matching;
  - apply keeping your own settings;
  - a second direction replacing the first;
  - stale entries being cleared;
  - the setup wizard never being reopened;
  - half-time labelling;
  - saving and deleting your own directions.
- **Installed app, BYOM Test:** "Best for this song" gave 120 BPM, mid-energy matches, and "Use this direction" marked the direction *In use*. Applying Lagos Nights and then Nordic Noir left Nordic Noir's brief, look, restrained acting and bible in place. The test project was reset afterwards.
- **Bug found and fixed during testing:** applying a direction to a project that never went through the setup wizard made the app reopen the wizard.

## Known limitations

- These are briefs, not reference images. The look comes from the look presets and the AI directions.
- Matching uses tempo, energy and section moods. It doesn't read lyrics or genre tags yet.

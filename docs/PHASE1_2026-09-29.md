# Phase 1: Bring Your Own Media and Production Bible basics (29 Sep 2026)

Every feature here is free: nothing is sent to any AI provider.

## What was built

- **Cut shots on the beat, no AI.** A project that has only a song gets a shot plan: one scene per song section, with a new shot every 4 bars, cut on the downbeat. It never overwrites an existing plan.
- **Your own images and clips as takes.**
  - For one shot: Shot panel → Your own media → *Use my image or clip*.
  - For many shots at once: top bar → *My media ▾* → *Add images and clips…*. Files go one per empty shot, in the order you chose, starting from the selected shot.
  - Imported files are copied into the project's `media/` folder.
  - Each import becomes a normal version of its shot and is approved automatically. Versions, the preview and export all use it.
  - Your own media is never sent to the AI review.
- **Your image as a keyframe.** An imported image can instead become the image an AI video starts from.
- **Camera moves on stills.** Choose from slow push in, pull out, pan left or right, tilt up or down, or hold still. The export and the live preview use the same move.
- **Moving stills from keyframes.** One click turns every shot that has an approved keyframe but no video into a moving still. The result is a complete draft video at no cost.
- **Transitions.** Each shot can enter with a cut, a dip to black or a flash of white (0.3 s each side).
- **Short clips** repeat to fill their shot.
- **Production Bible.** In the Reference library you can set:
  - World
  - Always (rules)
  - Palette
  - Never show

  These are saved in the project and added to every keyframe and video prompt.
- **Cost guard.** A shot with no description (for example one cut from the song alone) is never sent to a paid render. This is enforced in the engine as well as in the UI.

## Evidence

- **Tests:** 110 engine tests pass, 11 of them new. They include a real ffmpeg export of a song-only project with a moving image, a looped clip and dip-to-black transitions.
- **Run in the installed app** on the project "BYOM Test":
  - The song-only plan made 37 shots.
  - 6 images and 2 clips from Exit Plan were placed on the timeline.
  - The camera move was changed in the panel and saved to disk.
  - After restarting the app, every take, move and transition was still there.
  - The full 3:11 export at 1920×1080 took 70 s and cost nothing.
  - Checking frames of that export confirmed the push-in, the dip to black and the looped clips.

## Known limitations

- Transitions are dips (to black or white). There is no cross-dissolve between two shots yet.
- Shots can't be dragged to reorder or to change their length. Timing comes from the beat grid.
- Clips don't keep their own audio; the song is the soundtrack.
- The Mac build is not tested on hardware.

## Gate

The Bring Your Own Media path works end to end on Windows:

import → arrange → move → transition → preview → save → restart → export, at zero cost.
